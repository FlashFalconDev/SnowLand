**I agree to comply with the following rules throughout the skiing activity and course:**

1. 使用適合本人身材、能力及課程需求之滑雪裝備；如發現裝備有損壞、異常或故障情形，應立即停止使用，並通知教練、雪場工作人員或裝備提供單位處理。

   I will wear a helmet at all times during the course and use appropriate protective equipment according to the course, ski area conditions, and my personal needs.

3. 滑雪活動應注意同行人員及自身安全；未經教練同意，不擅自脫離課程範圍或單獨前往不熟悉之區域。

   I will remain mindful of my own safety and that of other participants. Without the instructor’s approval, I will not leave the designated lesson area or enter unfamiliar areas alone.

4. 應依自身技術程度、體能狀況及教練指示，選擇適合之雪道、地形及滑行方式，不從事明顯超出自身能力之滑行行為。

   I will select slopes, terrain, and skiing methods appropriate to my skill level and physical condition and will follow the instructor’s directions. I will not intentionally attempt skiing activities that are clearly beyond my ability.

5. 未經教練許可或帶領，不擅自進入陌生雪道、樹林、陡坡、雪場管制區、封閉雪道或其他具有較高危險性之區域。

   Without the instructor’s permission or supervision, I will not enter unfamiliar slopes, wooded areas, steep terrain, restricted areas, closed trails, or other areas presenting increased risks.

6. 僅使用自身目前技術能力可合理掌控之滑雪技巧，不任意嘗試明顯超出自身能力之動作。

   I will use skiing techniques that I can reasonably control at my current skill level and will not attempt maneuvers that are clearly beyond my ability.

7. 不任意停留於雪道中央、交會口、轉彎下坡處、坡頂後方、視線死角或其他可能妨礙滑行及增加碰撞風險之位置。

   I will not stop unnecessarily in the middle of a ski run, at intersections, on downhill turns, behind hill crests, in blind spots, or in any other location that may obstruct other skiers or increase the risk of collision.

   When using ski lifts, tow lifts, or other transportation facilities, I will board and exit in an orderly manner and follow the instructions of ski resort staff and instructors. I will not engage in horseplay, intentionally rock equipment, or engage in other conduct that may endanger myself or others.

4. ## **滑雪活動風險認知 Acknowledgement of Skiing Risks**

本人理解滑雪活動可能包括但不限於下列風險：  
I understand that skiing may involve, without limitation, the following risks:

1. 跌倒、滑倒或失去控制。  
   Falls, slips, or loss of control.  
2. 與其他滑雪者、教練、雪場設備、護欄、樹木、地形或其他物體發生碰撞。  
   Collisions with other skiers, instructors, ski resort equipment, barriers, trees, terrain, or other objects.  
3. 因冰面、積雪、坑洞、坡度、能見度、天候或雪況變化所產生之風險。  
   Risks arising from ice, accumulated snow, holes or uneven surfaces, slope gradient, visibility, weather, or changing snow conditions.  
4. 搭乘纜車、拖牽或其他雪場設備時可能發生之事故。  
   Accidents that may occur while using ski lifts, tow lifts, or other ski resort facilities.  
5. 因個人體力、健康狀況、技術程度或反應能力不足所產生之事故。  
   Accidents resulting from insufficient physical fitness, health condition, skill level, or reaction ability.  
6. 因其他滑雪者之行為而發生碰撞或受傷。  
   Collisions or injuries caused by the actions of other skiers.  
7. 其他滑雪活動本身可能合理發生之固有或不可完全避免之風險。  
   Other inherent or not entirely avoidable risks that may reasonably arise in connection with skiing activities.

**本人理解，即使教練提供適當安全說明及指導，仍無法完全排除上述風險。**  
**I understand that the above risks cannot be completely eliminated even when appropriate safety instructions and professional guidance are provided.**
